📋 How It Works

👤 Step 1 — Sign In

Choose your name from the list, then enter your student number and tap Sign In. Guest students will also enter their first name.

📚 Step 2 — Pick an Activity

Find your story and your list. Your teacher will tell you which activity to do today: Learn the Words, Yes or No, or Pick the Ending.

🎧 Hear It, Then Say It

In Learn the Words, you get four new words at a time. Each word has a card. Listen to the word, what it means, and a sentence. Then say the word out loud. After four cards, you answer a question about each of those words.

🔁 Step 3 — Tries

You get two tries for each activity. After two tries an activity is locked — only your teacher can unlock it.

📖 Reading Timer

When each question appears, the answer choices are locked for 12 seconds. Use that time to read the question and choices carefully before making your selection.

⏱️ 12 second read lock

⏳ Thinking Timer

After you confirm your answer, wait 6 seconds before moving on. Use that time to read what the word means — right or wrong!

⏳ 6 second pause

⏱ Overall Timer

A timer runs the whole time. It is not a countdown — there is no time limit. It only tracks how long you take so your teacher can see your progress.
